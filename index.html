How the score works — view assumptions

Three equally weighted indicators: recorded gross margin (40 points: 0% margin = 0; 40% or more = 40), valid-row share (30 points: 0% = 0; 100% = 30), and revenue diversification across products (30 points: a top-product share of 100% = 0; 40% or less = 30). Values between anchors scale linearly. Negative margins score zero on the margin component. These thresholds are invented for a classroom demonstration, not researched industry benchmarks. The score ignores cash flow, fixed expenses, taxes, liabilities, seasonality, and the correctness of source records. Missing or zero revenue means no score. Filtering changes the included rows and score. Review source data before acting.
